Student & Applicant Privacy Policy

How ABSS Nexus Academy protects student personal information, code submission repositories, and verifiable certificate credentials.

Home About Us Services / Programs Internship Details Contact Us Apply Now Student Login
Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Version: 2.0 (Academy Edition)

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an internship cohort or submit an application at ABSS Nexus Academy, we collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Full legal name, verified email address, mobile number (WhatsApp-enabled), and college/university affiliation.
  • Technical Artifacts: Git repository links, deployed project URLs, commit logs, and milestone submission notes.
  • Authentication Data: Cryptographically hashed passwords (salted with PBKDF2 at 100,000 iterations) or Firebase Google Authentication tokens.

2. How We Use Student Data

We process your information exclusively to administer academic cohorts, specifically:

  • Onboarding you into your chosen curriculum track and issuing your official Offer Letter.
  • Enabling 1-on-1 mentor code reviews and delivering project critique feedback.
  • Issuing tamper-proof completion certificates and operating the public credential verification registry.
  • Transmitting critical milestone deadlines and cohort announcements.

3. Verification Registry & Public Transparency

To protect credential integrity for employers and universities, our verification registry enables third-party credential checks via certificate ID or student email. Only the student's name, enrolled program track, issue date, and validation status are publicly verifiable. Private contact numbers, physical addresses, and financial logs are never publicly exposed.

4. Zero Third-Party Sale Policy

ABSS Nexus Academy has a strict zero-tolerance policy against commercial data brokers. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade student personal information to any third-party advertisers, lead brokers, or marketing networks.

5. Student Data Rights & Deletion

In accordance with digital data protection principles, students retain the right to request a copy of their submitted records or request account erasure. Verified completion certificates remain archived in the cryptographic ledger to prevent forgery or credential falsification.

6. Contacting the Privacy Officer
