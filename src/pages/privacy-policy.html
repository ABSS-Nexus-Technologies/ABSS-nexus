Privacy Policy: Transparent Data Collection & Handling Practices

This document outlines how ABSS Nexus Technologies collects, handles, and protects information submitted through our website and digital services.

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • Version: 2.0
Review-Ready Draft

Notice: Review-Ready Legal Draft

This privacy policy is a review-ready operational draft accurately describing this website’s technical data collection, browser storage, and Cloudflare edge processing practices. It is subject to formal legal counsel review prior to binding corporate execution.

1. Information We Collect

ABSS Nexus Technologies adheres to the principle of data minimization. We collect only information strictly necessary to evaluate project inquiries and maintain website security:

  • Contact Inquiry Information: When you complete our Project Inquiry Form, we collect your full name, business email address, telephone number, organization name, required service, estimated budget range, preferred timeline, and project description.
  • Technical Edge Telemetry: In the course of normal HTTP communication, our edge hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) automatically processes client IP addresses, browser user-agent headers, and HTTP request paths for DDoS mitigation, rate limiting, and anomaly detection.
  • Browser Client Storage: We utilize browser LocalStorage solely to maintain administrative authentication session tokens and temporary local inquiry submission queues.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Information submitted through our website is used strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To evaluate project feasibility, prepare technical scopes, and provide itemized quotations.
  • To communicate with prospective clients via email or phone regarding their inquiries.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate malicious traffic, bot spam, and security vulnerabilities.

We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize client inquiry information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure Processors

To maintain global edge availability and security, we partner with industry-standard infrastructure providers:

  • Cloudflare Inc.: Provides Content Delivery Network (CDN), DDoS mitigation, SSL encryption, and edge computing functions. Processing occurs under standard data protection agreements.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): Click-to-chat links redirect directly to the official WhatsApp protocol; communications over WhatsApp are governed by their respective end-to-end encrypted privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Security Controls

Client inquiries are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill project evaluation or ongoing contractual agreements. All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Administrative database access is restricted via cryptographically signed tokens.
