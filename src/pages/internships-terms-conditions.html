Terms & Conditions of Enrollment

Rules, expectations, intellectual property guidelines, and certificate issuance standards for all ABSS Nexus Academy participants.

Home About Us Services / Programs Internship Details Contact Us Apply Now Student Login
Last Updated: January 2026 • Applicable to: All Cohort Students & Applicants

1. Enrollment & Eligibility

By registering for an ABSS Nexus Academy internship cohort, you affirm that all submitted information (including your name, academic enrollment status, and email) is true and accurate. Participation requires basic computer literacy, access to a laptop or computer, and an active internet connection.

2. Academic Integrity & Code Originality

ABSS Nexus Academy values authentic engineering competence above all else:

  • Plagiarism: Submitting clone repos without meaningful contribution or claiming other developers' work as your own will result in immediate disqualification.
  • AI Tooling: Students are encouraged to use modern tools (such as AI code assistants) responsibly, but must demonstrate clear personal comprehension of all architectural choices and commit history.

3. Project Milestones & Code Reviews

Cohorts operate with flexible milestone deadlines. To be eligible for final certificate issuance, students must complete the mandatory milestone deliverables specified in their curriculum syllabus and implement feedback received during code review cycles.

4. Certificate Issuance & Revocation Policy

Completion certificates are issued strictly upon successful project sign-off. ABSS Nexus Academy reserves the explicit right to revoke any certificate if it is subsequently determined that the work was obtained through fraudulent means, impersonation, or academic dishonesty. Revoked credentials are immediately flagged as invalid in our public verification registry.

5. Intellectual Property Rights

Students retain full intellectual property ownership of the code they write during open-source capstone projects. ABSS Nexus Academy retains ownership of all proprietary learning materials, curriculum frameworks, and verification software.

6. Honest Disclosure: Educational Nature of Program

ABSS Nexus Academy is a skill-building, virtual engineering training and portfolio-development program. In accordance with honest consumer practices, participation in this program does not constitute an offer of commercial employment, nor do we guarantee salary packages or direct job placements.

7. Amendments & Jurisdiction

ABSS Nexus Academy reserves the right to update these terms to reflect evolving technical frameworks. Any dispute arising out of enrollment shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.
